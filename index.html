About us

Neuvents is a Sofia based company that specializes in organizing IT events and conferences. The company was founded in 2016 and has organized several conferences – Euruko 2016, PartialConf and Balkan Ruby. Neuvents also runs the local Ruby meetup, called Ruby Banitsa.

Conference

Balkan Ruby

Balkan Ruby is a brand new conference, dedicated to Ruby and satellite technologies. We aim to introduce the local communities to each other and invite companies and developers from all over Europe to visit the Balkan region, learn new technologies and share ideas with us.

Conference

Partial Conf

Partial is a conference about modern functional programming languages and technologies for the web. Think Erlang, Elixir, Clojure, ClojureScript, Haskell, Elm, PureScript, F#, React and many more. It’s a conference that can get you outside your comfort zone and let you learn something new.
