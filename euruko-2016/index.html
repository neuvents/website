Euruko 2016

About the conference

EuRuKo is an annual non-profit tech conference with an accent on Ruby and satellite technologies. It is the second largest Ruby community gathering in the world. In 2016 we had the honor of organizing EuRuKo in Sofia, Bulgaria. It's the event that got us started organizing community conferences and a cause that's dear to our hearts.
